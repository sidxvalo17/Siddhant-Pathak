01 / Introduction

Technology should be accessible to everyone.

My work is centred on helping people feel confident using technology and discovering what they can create with it.

As an AI Trainer, I introduce college graduates to practical ways of working with low-code and no-code tools. These platforms make it possible to design workflows and build applications without needing extensive programming experience.

Through demonstrations and hands-on learning, I encourage students to explore AI-powered tools, solve everyday problems, and develop digital skills they can apply in their careers.

I believe the best technology education is practical, approachable, and focused on helping people turn curiosity into capability.

What I aim to encourage

Learning by doing, asking better questions, and using technology creatively.

✓Confidence with emerging technologies
✓Practical digital and problem-solving skills
✓Creative thinking through application building

Introduction

I am currently a student at KR Mangalam University, pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Journalism and Mass Communication.

My curiosity lives where media, visual storytelling and creative communication meet. I’m drawn to the many ways a story can be told, in a photograph, a news report, a screenplay or a film, and to what each medium makes possible.

Studying journalism and mass communication helps me see how storytelling, society, creativity and the media industry shape one another. Filmmaking is where that curiosity runs deepest: following a story from its first idea to the moment it becomes a meaningful visual experience.

Hobbies

The things I do for the joy of it, and that quietly teach me how stories work.

Photography

Photography is one of my favourite creative pursuits. I enjoy capturing moments, observing everyday surroundings and finding stories within ordinary scenes. It lets me experiment with composition, lighting, perspective and visual aesthetics, and it has taught me how images can carry emotion and story without a single word.

Screenplay Writing

I enjoy exploring screenplay writing and developing stories for the screen: creating characters, constructing narratives, building conflict and writing meaningful dialogue. I’m especially drawn to psychological themes, human emotion, character development and unconventional narratives.

Watching Films and Shows

Watching films and television is more than entertainment for me. I explore different genres, storytelling techniques, cinematic styles, performances and directorial approaches. Every film is a chance to see how a director communicates ideas and emotions through visual storytelling.

Interests

Where my curiosity is heading as a student, and as a future filmmaker.

Studying Films

I have a deep interest in studying cinema beyond its entertainment value. I enjoy analysing narrative structure, symbolism, character arcs, editing patterns, sound design, colour grading and directorial choices.

I’m particularly interested in how filmmakers use cinematic technique to communicate complex human emotions, philosophical ideas and social realities.

Cinematography

Cinematography is one of my strongest creative interests. I’m fascinated by how camera movement, framing, lighting, composition, colour and angle shape the way an audience perceives a story.

I enjoy exploring the visual language of cinema, and understanding how cinematographers turn written narratives into compelling visual experiences.
